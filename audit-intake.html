How to send pages safely

  • HTML snippets, not whole pages. In Chrome DevTools, right-click the target in Elements, then Copy > Copy outerHTML. Copy from the nearest container that holds the target and the label or heading next to it (usually 2 to 4 levels up).
  • Redact first. Replace names, emails, order numbers, prices, tokens and hidden input values with REDACTED. Keep tag names, attributes, classes, test ids and visible labels, since that's what locators use.
  • Never send logins. Please don't send logins, passwords, API keys or access to your systems. We don't need them.
  • Numbered screenshots. Attach full-page screenshots with the targets circled or numbered to match your locator list.

Your files are deleted 30 days after delivery (or on refund), and they're never used in marketing, demos or public examples without your written permission.

If we're at capacity, we'll email you a start date within 24 hours, and the 48-hour clock starts then.
