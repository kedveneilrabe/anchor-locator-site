What Anchor Locator is

Anchor Locator is a browser extension that generates XPath locators from a target element and an anchor element you choose, and includes a Recorder that exports test scripts for Playwright, Selenium and Cypress. All locator features are free. The Recorder is free for 10 recorded steps in total (a lifetime allowance that does not reset; assertions count as steps), and Anchor Locator Pro removes that limit.
