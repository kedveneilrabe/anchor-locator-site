The short version

  • Full refund within 14 days of purchase, no questions asked.
  • Applies to both Pro Monthly ($8/month) and Pro Lifetime ($49).
  • You keep everything you've recorded.

Our 14-day guarantee

If Anchor Locator Pro isn't right for you, ask for a refund within 14 days of your purchase and you'll get your money back in full. You don't need to give a reason.

  • Pro Lifetime ($49): refundable in full within 14 days of purchase.
  • Pro Monthly ($8/month): refundable in full within 14 days of the payment. You can also cancel at any time to stop future renewals.

How to request a refund

Contact us with the email address you used at checkout (or your order number), and say you'd like a refund. Payments are handled by Lemon Squeezy, our merchant of record, so you can also request a refund through Lemon Squeezy using the details in your order receipt.

How refunds are processed

Refunds are issued by Lemon Squeezy to the original payment method. Once the refund is issued, it usually appears within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank or card provider.

What happens after a refund

Your Pro license is deactivated and the Recorder returns to the free allowance. Everything you've recorded stays in the extension and can still be exported, and every free locator feature keeps working.

The free plan

All locator features and 10 Recorder steps are free, so there's nothing to refund on the free plan.

Questions

If something isn't working, contact us; we may be able to fix it. Either way, the refund stays available within the 14 days.
